## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist and M.S. Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Maryland, College Park**, interested in building intelligent systems that turn data into useful, measurable outcomes.

Before starting my master's, I worked as a **Junior Data Scientist at Applied Data Finance**, where I developed machine learning systems for U.S. consumer credit risk and worked across the full ML lifecycle — from feature engineering and model development to validation, scoring, QA, and deployment.

My interests span:

- 📈 Time-Series Forecasting
- 🧩 ML Engineering & Production Systems

Currently exploring the intersection of **data, machine learning, generative AI, and intelligent systems**.
